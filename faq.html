Put your sites and chargers online

How do I add a site?
  1. Open Sites and click + Add site.
  2. Enter the site name, choose the type (complex, building, parking, parking spot or standalone site) and fill in the address and city. Start typing the address and pick a suggestion: the map position is filled in for you.
  3. Save. The site now appears in your list and you can add chargers to it.

The map position is what shows your site on the customer map, so it is worth getting right.

How do I add a charger?
  1. Open Chargers and click + Add charger.
  2. Choose the site, give the charger a name (for example Charger C1), choose the connector type and enter its maximum power in kW (for example 7.4).
  3. Choose the OCPP version of your charger (1.6J for most chargers).
  4. Click Generate connection details, then follow the next section to enter them in your charger.
When do drivers see my charger on the public map?

Only when Show on the public site (charge.plugndrive.co.za) is ticked for that charger. It is off by default, so you can test everything first.

A hidden charger still works through its own QR code or link, which is handy for demos and for private use.

Tick the box once the charger is connected and answering, and you are ready to receive drivers.

Connect a charger

In short, three things go into your charger:
Server URL
the WebSocket URL generated by the console
Charger ID
the last part of that URL (this is also the username, if your charger asks for one)
Password
the Auth password generated by the console
What do I need to enter in my charger?

After you click Generate connection details in the console, you get two things:

  • WebSocket URL, which looks like wss://ocpp.plugndrive.co.za/your-charger-123. The last part is your charger's identifier.
  • Auth password, a short code made for this charger only.

Enter them in the settings of your charger (its app, web page or installer menu). The names differ from one brand to another:

  • Server address (also called CSMS URL, central system URL or backend URL): the WebSocket URL.
  • Charge point ID (or charger ID, station ID): the last part of the URL, for example your-charger-123.
  • Password (or authorization key, Basic Auth password): the Auth password.
  • Username, if your charger asks for one: always the charge point ID.
  • Protocol: OCPP 1.6J over WebSocket.
Some chargers want the full URL in one field. Others want the address without the identifier in one field and the identifier in another. If your charger has a separate ID field, give it the base address wss://ocpp.plugndrive.co.za and the identifier on its own.
How do I know it works?
  1. After entering the details in your charger, restart it so that it connects.
  2. In the console, click Save and test connection. It saves the charger, waits for it to connect, then checks that it answers a live request.
  3. When you see The charger is online and answering, you are done. The charger appears on the Dashboard with its live status.
The test says the charger has not connected yet

The charger is saved as Offline and switches to Available by itself as soon as it connects, so nothing is lost. Check these points, then test again:

  • The URL is typed exactly as shown, starting with wss://, with no extra space at the end.
  • The password is the one shown for this charger. If you generated the details more than once, use the latest ones.
  • The charger is set to OCPP 1.6J and has been restarted after the change.
  • The charger has internet access (Wi-Fi, network cable or SIM card with data).
  • The date and time of the charger are correct (some chargers refuse secure connections when their clock is wrong).

Still stuck? Use the Help button and tell us the charger name and brand.

My charger goes Offline from time to time

A charger shows Offline when it loses its internet connection, and it comes back by itself once the connection returns. A charge that was running keeps going and is settled normally when the charger reconnects.

If it happens often, check the signal at the charger (Wi-Fi strength or SIM coverage) and make sure only one device uses the same charger identifier.

Get paid

How do I connect my bank account?
  1. Open Settings and go to Payouts (Paystack).
  2. Enter your business name, choose your bank and enter your account number.
  3. Click Connect.

Once it is connected, the page shows the account name and the last four digits of the account number. Your payouts are sent to this account whenever Plug n Drive pays out your revenue.

Settings is available to the account administrator of the company. If you do not see it, ask your administrator or contact us.
How do I change my bank account?

In Settings, under Payouts (Paystack), click Change bank details, fill in the new details and click Connect.

The connection is refused

Check that the account number has no spaces or dashes, that you chose the right bank, and that the account is in the same name as the business. If the message stays, send it to us with the Help button and we will look at it.

Where can I see what I earned?

Your sessions and the amounts they brought in are listed in the console, and the balance and payments are shown in Settings under Balance & payments. For anything about the timing of a payout, use the Help button.
